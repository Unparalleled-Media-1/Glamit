GLAM AI is a personal AI beauty, grooming and styling companion: **Understand → Personalize → Try → Get This Look**. The six entry points route to understanding features, event styling, recreating inspiration, hairstyle/makeup exploration, owned wardrobe and product/professional sourcing. Personal navigation remains Home · Analyze · AI Studio · AI Advisor · Profile. ## Implemented interactive slice

- Compact mobile home with two immediate actions (Face Analysis and Personal Stylist), six goals, first-use profile introduction and returning-plan summary. Desktop retains editorial imagery; mobile uses a small portrait accent. Fixed browser nav reserves content space and uses `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)` with viewport-fit cover. Native uses Expo-compatible safe-area context instead of guessed iOS/Android insets; nav lives outside the scrolling content. Physical devices remain unverified.
- Advisor, event and inspiration routes share a user-authored conversational brief. Occasion, user-provided theme, date, needed-by date, total budget, comfort/coverage/colors, sourcing preference and shopping destination survive internal navigation. Messages are explicitly session drafts; no model reply, official theme, invitation extraction or recommendation is fabricated. Web accepts rights-gated image/PDF references; native accepts rights-gated invitation/inspiration images. Object URLs/cached copies are cleared on removal/unmount; references do not become durable profile data.
- `/builder` edits hair, makeup, clothing, accessories and professional services independently. Clearing one component preserves the others. Save creates a snapshot; opening/editing a saved look updates that saved identity only after Save. Selected Studio directions can explicitly enter the builder. Decorative material swatches/editorial images are not simulations or product shades.
- `/wardrobe` supports add/edit/delete text item notes and reuse as the clothing component without erasing hair/makeup. Recognition and image-backed persistent wardrobe ingestion remain deferred.
- `/complete` is Get This Look: component breakdown, destination/currency, unknown total, organic and separately labeled sponsored no-match sections and professional/tailor brief route. Existing qualified matching/card contracts remain available, but this disconnected client has no verified offers or confirmed handoffs/quotes/bookings.
- Profile supports optional name, voluntary style/shopping preferences, current event plan, current/saved looks and wardrobe metadata. Explicit **Remember** opt-in stores allowlisted text on this browser/device; off removes durable memory. Stored data is bounded and versioned; malformed/unsupported data restores safely. Edit/delete/reset are available. Conversations, photos, attachments, auth credentials, inferred identity, analysis reports and grants are excluded. Google/Supabase account sync and persistent approved real reports require the deferred connection stage. Shared-device storage is explicitly optional. This is not encrypted account storage or a substitute for authenticated server ownership controls.

## Commercial policy

One fixed central **10% eligible item-sale commission**, independent of mobile/web, discovery journey, organic/sponsored placement, merchant category or Snap & List origin. Eligible item value is after discounts and excludes separately charged delivery, tax, processing and advertising fees. Display no unexpected customer surcharge. Cancelled/uncompleted sales earn no commission. Partial/full refunds reverse the corresponding fee; half-up rounding is per line, and cumulative remaining-value calculation eliminates split-refund rounding residue. `commerce_policy.py` implements this pure server policy with tests; it does **not** implement a settlement ledger or accept browser-computed revenue.

A future verified order adapter must lock the canonical line/refund record, deduplicate signed events and reconcile to the cumulative target. Full checkout, multi-merchant orders, KYC/KYB, inventory holds, taxes, returns, disputes, reconciliation, statements and payouts remain unimplemented. External affiliate checkout needs an actual attributable reporting/settlement contract; a referral cannot automatically deduct 10%. Service commission is **unapproved/unset**, not silently 10%. Advertising fees are separate.

## Location and privacy

Manual shopping destination takes precedence over future saved address, permission-based approximate device location and approximate IP suggestions. No location is detected in this build. No precise location permission is requested. A traveler may set a different shopping destination/currency. Selected currency is a preference, not FX conversion or market activation. Existing catalog matching enforces country/currency and latest default-deny market policies; city coverage, delivery deadline and local radius evidence need later contracts. No unavailable merchant is represented as local.

Private face reports, wardrobe, event conversations and voluntary coverage/religious/cultural preferences never become advertiser attributes. Never infer religion or other sensitive characteristics from photos. Sponsored serving, geographic attribution and reporting remain unavailable until market/age/privacy, budget, delivery and advertiser qualification are implemented. An eligible bid cannot override relevance, variant, stock, fulfillment or timing.

## Quality and release gates

Automated browser journeys cover first visitor, first face preparation, returning profile, event brief, original-preserving makeover, independent builder, Get This Look, Snap & List and denied workspace switching. Keyboard/focus, bounded uploads, original image preservation, durable metadata exclusions/deletion, 320–1920px overflow, 320/390×640 primary action reachability, fixed-nav geometry, last-content reachability, offline guards and reduced motion have repeatable checks. Native shares the domain model; TypeScript and iOS/Android Hermes exports are build checks only.

Human usability/task-completion studies, a full WCAG audit, screen-reader/device keyboard/notch checks, provider-backed loading/success/retry/cancellation, inclusive analysis/makeover quality and **real matching accuracy** remain open. Fixture eligibility tests prove rules, not retrieval accuracy or customer fit. No prototype is approved or market-ready simply because it builds or passes browser tests. See [phased gap backlog](goal-led-backlog.md) and [validation](../validation.md).
