# Look recommendation ranking and commercial rules

V2 rollout/privacy authority: [Master PRD v2](master-prd-v2.md), [global policy](global-policy-architecture.md) and [commerce operations](commerce-operations-v2.md). First-price auction design below remains the eventual baseline; initial real-demand pilots may use reviewed fixed sponsorship and defer complex auctions. Unknown/teen/child ad contexts are suppressed in the initial policy. Even explicit style context may require suppression if sensitive; no targeting from inferred identity, private memory/wardrobe/twin/images/traits, age/guardian evidence or user-level intelligence. [Companion intelligence](style-companion-extension.md) requires consented aggregate projections and anti-differencing. No capability is activated by this design revision.

Status: proposed, version every applied policy. No production auction/billing system exists. Read alongside the existing money ledger, attribution and privacy designs.

## Separate analysis, organic matching and paid delivery

Decomposition outputs are immutable and hashed; model inputs exclude campaigns, bids, commission rates and advertiser identities. Product and professional matching operate on that revision. Advertising neither edits the revision nor substitutes paid results into the organic rank. Re-running with a different campaign configuration must produce identical analysis and organic ordering for the same fixed catalog/model/policy inputs. Availability changes can legitimately change matching and must be versioned independently.

Hard eligibility precedes scoring: approved seller/listing/rights; selected destination/country supported; required compatibility constraints; legal product category; price/currency evidence; delivery eligibility; allowed inventory state/freshness; service capability and location/date constraints. A nonmatching sponsored offer cannot buy eligibility. Private user constraints may exclude candidates inside matching without revealing those constraints to advertisers. Unknown fit/material/service skill prompts clarification, not an exact-match claim. Allergy-sensitive suitability requires confirmed product evidence and warnings; never certify safety from an image.

Use catalog category/attribute retrieval plus multilingual lexical and embedding search; treat feed text as data, never model instructions. Prefer structured evidence for exact attributes; model similarity cannot override a contradictory size/material/texture constraint. The model proposes catalog IDs; the server resolves and verifies them. Only verified source provenance supports `Exact item`; `Similar style` and `Needs confirmation` are explicit alternatives.

Proposed organic product score, each factor normalized to [0,1]:

`O = .35 compatibility + .20 style_similarity + .15 evidence_freshness + .10 landed_value + .10 merchant_fulfillment_quality + .10 regional_preference`

Professional score: `.35 verified_capability + .20 confirmed_service_fit + .15 availability + .10 travel_fit + .10 evidenced_service_quality + .10 value`. Cold-start merchants get a neutral prior and an exploration quota among eligible organic results, not fabricated quality ratings. Trust criteria cannot be reduced to paid spend. Regional preference comes from explicit user choices and approved nonsensitive context.

Missing-factor handling is policy-versioned: unknown essential evidence excludes; unknown landed shipping cost receives a conservative value factor and a visible unknown-cost indicator, not a zero shipping assumption. Display a separate coverage/confidence measure; do not hide uncertainty inside a scalar rank. For cross-currency comparison, use a dated server FX quote for display, retain original currency and suppress `lowest total` where inputs are incomplete. Deduplicate identical offers and limit seller repetition without excluding a uniquely compatible match. Tie-break deterministically by evidence quality then stable identifier. Track relevance, coverage, diversity, catalog freshness and user-confirmed match quality separately from revenue.

Assembly scoring maximizes required-component coverage, compatibility and user preferences within budget and delivery constraints, accounting for kits, pack quantities, owned items and substitutions. Do not optimize for commission or ad spend. A transparent heuristic is sufficient initially; flag unsatisfied dependencies rather than presenting a mathematically optimal claim. Paid offers can be explicitly selected by the user into an assembly without changing organic ranking.

## Sponsored eligibility, score and auction

Propose a **first-price CPC auction**, one winner per dedicated component-group slot and at most two sponsored cards per result page initially. Each panel is visibly separate, labeled `Sponsored`, and gives advertiser identity and a nonsensitive explanation. A sponsored collection uses an explicit contract/disclosed sponsorship; its flat-fee billing is separate from CPC. No undisclosed pay-to-include organic placements.

Require verified advertiser/merchant link, approved campaign/creative/landing domain, country/product compliance, schedule, funded budget or approved credit line, fresh purchasable offer, compatible component coverage, and privacy-safe placement context. Service ads require verified service pricing basis and eligible area; a slot claim requires an actual availability response. Apply user consent/opt-outs, frequency caps and merchant/campaign deduplication before auction. Use coarse category/page context only; no facial images, measurements, inferred color/texture/body type, private prompts, sensitive look embeddings or private look IDs in advertiser-facing requests/reports. Do not permit advertiser-created target predicates that reconstruct these attributes.

Sponsored relevance `q` uses only approved nonsensitive contextual/catalog features, independent of bid: `.60 generic category match + .25 supported offer attributes + .15 evidence quality`, with proposed minimum `q ≥ .75`. Private compatibility is a prior exclusion decision within matching; its reason/features never enter advertiser logs or scoring features. Disable auction for contexts whose use would disclose sensitive attributes. Do not use individualized predicted click propensity at launch. All scores are proposals requiring offline qualification, not measured probabilities.

For an eligible campaign let `b` be its maximum CPC bid in the auction's billing currency, integer minor units. Enforce placement minimum `floor` in that currency. Rank by `q × b`; use exact fixed-point arithmetic, then `q`, then a deterministic recorded tie key. Winner pays **its submitted bid for a validated unique click**, never more than `b`; losing bidders pay nothing. This is first price, not a quality-adjusted second-price formula. Show the pricing rule in the advertiser UI. No cross-currency auction initially: campaigns fund a supported country/currency wallet; display FX cannot alter charges.

Example: A has q=.90, b=200 minor units, score=180; B has q=.80, b=250, score=200; B wins and pays 250 for a valid click. A with q=.70 is ineligible regardless of bid. Frequency/budget restrictions can select the next eligible candidate or return no ad; organic results remain available.

### Atomic serve and billing protocol

1. Fetch eligible candidates and policy version. Non-financial pacing can gate candidates against local-day budget/time elapsed; pacing never bypasses hard limits. Refresh eligibility before commit.
2. In a transaction lock the advertiser wallet/credit-line allocation and campaign total plus local-day budget rows in stable order. Check `spent + held + bid ≤ cap` for each, including account limits. Check frequency counters/holds. Reserve the full maximum click charge for each delivered token and a frequency slot atomically, with unique request/slot IDs. If a candidate loses a concurrency race, try the next eligible candidate; no optimistic overspend.
3. Issue a signed delivery token bound to campaign, offer revision, slot, accepted CPC, policy version, expiry and a server-held pseudonymous session reference. No private look/profile data is encoded. Proposed validity: 5 minutes. Reserve counts conservatively against frequency at delivery; verified viewability converts the hold to counted exposure. Undisplayed holds expire. Suggested caps: 3/campaign/session/day and 10/advertiser/session/7 days. For consenting signed-in users, a server pseudonymous account key can enforce cross-device caps; no fingerprinting. Anonymous caps are best effort and described as such. A privacy-compatible cap mechanism is required or suppress ads.
4. A deliberate interaction submits the token before expiry. Deduplicate to at most one billable click per delivery and at most one billable campaign/offer click per pseudonymous subject per 30 minutes under the proposed policy. A signed token proves delivery lineage, not a human click; apply replay, bot, velocity and interaction checks. Viewability is separately reported; it is not proof of a valid click. Billable interactions do not have to wait for client viewability telemetry that may be absent.
5. Accepted clicks transition the reservation to `pending_validation` atomically, preserving its funds. Set a validation deadline (proposed 24 hours); charge only if validated, otherwise release or mark nonbillable by the deadline. Record charge and balanced postings once with `(delivery_id, charge_type)` uniqueness. No click or late click releases/no-charges the hold; an expired ad link can still navigate but is nonbillable unless explicitly re-served before interaction. Never debit an expired released reservation retroactively. Marked-invalid later events receive compensating advertiser credits, not edited journal entries.
6. A reservation is charged against the campaign local-day bucket at delivery time even if validation crosses midnight; funds remain held in that bucket until finalized. Billing pauses stop new delivery and honor disclosed valid in-flight tokens. Reject immediate cap reductions below spent+held with 409; allow pause and a scheduled lower cap once released holds permit it, never silently violate the cap invariant. Emergency fraud stops can cancel tokens nonbillably. Campaign timezone is fixed after activation; DST uses IANA calendar dates, not assumed 24-hour days. TTL cleanup and reconciliation repair stranded reservations without creating spend.

Seven-day frequency limits use rolling windows over durable counted exposures and outstanding holds, with bounded time buckets and exact boundary handling, not fixed calendar weeks that allow boundary bursts. Daily caps use the campaign-local day. Counters are repairable transactional projections; expiration cannot release a counted exposure early.

Reservation-at-delivery prevents overspend but temporarily ties up more funds than expected clicks consume; this tradeoff may reduce delivery for small budgets. Limit outstanding tokens, monitor hold-to-spend ratio and tune TTL only through approved policy versions. Do not replace hard holds with assumed CTR to improve fill. Account wallet availability also accounts for funds reserved by other campaigns. Sponsored collection obligations cannot reuse already-reserved CPC funds.

## Attribution, commissions and auditable revenue

Record delivery, viewability, click validation, affiliate redirect, checkout, verified payment, fulfillment, refund and dispute as separate events. Use server IDs, event schema versions, event-time and received-time, signed external postbacks, inbox deduplication and outbox publication. Client `purchase` events never establish revenue. Preserve externally sourced event IDs and payload hashes with limited sensitive payload retention.

Proposed paid-ad reporting: last eligible validated click within seven days, no view-through, frozen at checkout against a versioned attribution rule. Creator affiliate attribution is a separate seven-day last eligible click/contract rule. A conversion can be reported as ad-assisted and commission-bearing, but revenue is counted once in overall merchant totals and at most one creator commission is paid per eligible order line. Show overlapping channel definitions rather than summing both as unique sales. Discount-code precedence and funding source must be agreed before launch; default no duplicate commission. Consent loss/anonymous gaps produce `unattributed`, not probabilistic identity stitching.

Multi-seller and partial orders attribute eligible lines, not unrelated basket value. Proposed ROAS numerator is captured attributable line subtotal net of discounts, refunds and tax, excluding shipping; publish that basis. Separate `ordered`, `paid`, `fulfilled` and `net after refunds`. Do not imply incremental causation. External conversions need a contracted verified feed; until then report clicks only. No fabricated zero-conversion certainty where tracking is unavailable: show `not measurable`.

CPC charges, platform marketplace fees, seller payables, creator commissions and flat collection sponsorship revenue use distinct ledger accounts/business IDs. Budgets/deposits are liabilities until earned. Marketplace commission uses accepted price/fee agreement and server rounding. Creator payable waits for the agreed return/fraud period; refund/chargeback reversals are linked compensating entries, with funded recovery policy if already paid. Do not debit creators arbitrarily outside signed terms. CPC is not automatically refunded because a shopper returns a legitimate purchase; fraud/invalid clicks follow the advertiser adjustment policy.

Fraud controls: traffic source/velocity checks, replay rejection, bot/automation heuristics, fake merchant/price manipulation monitoring, affiliate self-dealing/commission loops, collusive advertiser click farms, duplicate postbacks, abnormal refund rates, and payout change review. Risk holds are explainable and appealable; raw device/identity signals have strict retention/access controls, never face recognition. Delayed invalid traffic updates reports and credits with `as_of`/restatement versions.

Daily reconciliation checks delivery holds→click decisions→ad charges→money postings→invoice totals; catalog clicks→verified conversions→line allocation→commission journals→payouts; PSP funding/settlement→wallet balances. Require balanced books, duplicate detection, explainable timing differences and owned exception queues. Delivery replay uses retained approved catalog/rank/bid/policy hashes without retaining facial attributes. Critical inconsistencies halt affected spend/settlement.

## Advertiser dashboard specification

| Area | Required controls and data |
|---|---|
| Organization | Verification/KYC status, linked merchant/agency authorization, scoped admin/campaign/catalog/analyst/finance roles, MFA and audit |
| Catalog | API connector/CSV/manual submission, feed mapping/dry-run, row errors, source freshness, stock/price/delivery status, disapproved offers and appeals; no consumer media |
| Campaigns | Objective, product/service/collection source, approved countries/currency, coarse contextual placements, CPC bid/floor explanation, creative preview with `Sponsored`, start/end/timezone, review/version state |
| Budgets | Wallet/credit-line funding, total/daily caps, held/pending/charged/available amounts, pacing, frequency caps, alerts, pause/stop, cap reduction effect on in-flight tokens |
| Performance | Deliveries vs viewable impressions, unique validated clicks, pending/invalid clicks, CPC spend, CTR denominator, captured/net conversions, CPA/ROAS basis, attribution window and unmeasurable traffic |
| Reporting | Aggregate country/day/campaign/placement/product breakdowns where privacy thresholds pass, currency/timezone/as-of, delay/restatements, signed expiring export and access audit |
| Finance | Posted charges, invalid-click credits, funding, invoices/tax, payment failures, reconciliation exceptions and dispute/appeal evidence with private user data redacted |
| Collections | Rights, creator/merchant contracts, collection sponsorship dates, explicit labels, flat-fee obligation and prorating/cancellation terms, separation from CPC charges |

Campaign editing creates a new reviewable revision; material creative/landing/target changes require approval. Pause takes effect for new delivery immediately after the authoritative state change; explain outstanding reservations. Dashboards use authenticated server APIs and tenant predicates on every report/export. No individual user drill-down, private look thumbnails, sensitive audiences, exact address or per-user click timeline. Apply minimum cohort 50 plus query-budget/differencing protections to audience/conversion breakdowns. Advertisers may view exact own aggregate spend and billing adjustments without receiving user identifiers; suppression must not hide a financial reconciliation failure.
