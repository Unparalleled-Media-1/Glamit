1. **Core UX review and account persistence:** complete human task studies and device accessibility, connect selected identity/database/private storage; implement canonical profile/wardrobe/report lifecycle before account-memory claims.
2. **Qualified AI and verified supply:** qualify analysis/try-on/advisor and invitation extraction; wire authorized catalog/media; evaluate diverse user cohorts, identity preservation and real product matching, local coverage and deadlines. No made-up substitutes on empty search.
3. **Controlled commercial pilot:** finalize seller/facilitator obligations and signed partner contracts; implement one order/commission/settlement ledger, inventory/booking holds, KYC, returns/disputes and full sandbox-to-controlled-live reconciliation before collecting money.
4. **Marketplace growth:** tailor/rental production management, creator attribution, alerts/reviews, eligible local sponsored serving, consented analytics and fraud monitoring after core commercial evidence. Complex advertising optimization and B2B licensing follow measured demand.

This is the complete gap assessment, not a claim that every module is delivered. Existing secure foundations are retained; no duplicate identities, ledgers or migration history are introduced.
