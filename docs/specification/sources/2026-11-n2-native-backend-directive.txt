### What N1 actually achieved

The most valuable improvement is the camera/photo-picker safety fix. It prevents stale camera permissions, withdrawn consent and asynchronous picker results from causing inappropriate photo retention or screen transitions.

Codex also reports that the real native applications compiled, installed and launched on iOS and Android CI environments. That is substantially stronger evidence than the earlier browser gallery.

But the report identifies the biggest remaining problem:

The application entry point does not supply the `CompanionConnection` required by the consumer service hooks.

Consequently, the app may display native screens while its core analysis, AI generation, weather and marketplace operations remain unavailable.

That is now the primary architectural issue to resolve.
