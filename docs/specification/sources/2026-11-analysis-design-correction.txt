## 1. What I like about the current designs

The editorial imagery, neutral background, restrained colors and serif headings create a premium brand identity. The layout is also reasonably uncluttered, which is important for an AI app.

The desktop Complete the Look page has a particularly useful three-column structure: visual preview, look components and a summary panel. I would preserve that layout for the optional shopping experience.

I also like the honest labels such as Availability unverified, Quote needed and Unconfirmed. They prevent the interface from pretending live commerce integrations already exist.

## 2. The biggest problems

| Mobile hero: “Find Your Next Look” | Promotes fashion discovery rather than analysis | “Discover What Suits You”                                      |

The most serious issue is that a first-time visitor cannot immediately tell that GLAM AI analyzes their facial features and provides personalized beauty advice.

That is what must change.

## 4. What should happen after Face Analysis?

This is where the app should become genuinely compelling.

1. Personal Analysis Report

   Face shape, eye shape, brow and lip features, skin tone and estimated undertone.

2. Personal Color Analysis

   Suggested color palettes, makeup shades, hair colors and clothing colors.

3. Your Personalized Glow-Up Guide

   Practical makeup techniques, hairstyles, grooming and routines based on user-selected goals.

4. AI Makeover Preview

   Try the recommendations on your own photograph and compare variations.

5. Optional Complete the Look

   Discover relevant products, sponsored matches and professionals only when the user wants to recreate the look.

The analysis must not make medical diagnoses, rank attractiveness or treat estimated facial characteristics as infallible.

## 7. Exact instruction to send to the UI/UX designer or Codex

GLAM AI — UI/UX Correction Required

# GLAM AI — UI/UX Correction Required

The current visual designs are approved as a general design direction, but the product hierarchy and navigation are not approved.

The designs currently overemphasize fashion shopping and Complete the Look.

GLAM AI's primary purpose is to compete with and improve upon Glam Up – Perfect Your Look through inclusive AI facial analysis, personal color analysis, personalized glow-up guidance, makeup matching and realistic makeover previews.

## Required changes

1. Preserve the premium editorial aesthetic, typography, neutral backgrounds, restrained accent colors and general visual quality.

2. Change mobile primary navigation to:
   Home | Analyze | AI Studio | AI Advisor | Profile.

3. Make Analyze My Face the main homepage CTA.

4. Add prominent Face Analysis and Color Analysis entry points.

5. Replace the shopping-first mobile hero with a personal analysis and glow-up hero.

6. Redesign the desktop homepage around Face Analysis, Color Analysis, Glow-Up Guide and AI Studio.

7. Move Shop the Look, Fashion, Products and Professionals into secondary navigation or contextual experiences.

8. Retain the existing Complete the Look desktop design as a secondary commerce page.

9. Add a TikTok-style account/workspace switcher in Profile for approved Creator, Merchant, Professional, Advertiser and Affiliate roles.

10. Produce complete mobile and desktop screens for selfie capture, facial analysis, color analysis, personal report, personalized glow-up guide, makeup matching, makeover comparison and saved results.

11. Include all empty, loading, failed, permission-denied, offline and success states.

12. Preserve accessibility, global inclusivity, age-specific safeguards and private-by-default user imagery.

## Approval requirement

Do not proceed to the full marketplace or advertising UI until the core consumer analysis and makeover journeys have complete, approved interactive prototypes.

The flagship journey must be:

Start Face Analysis → Receive Personal Report → Explore Color Analysis → Generate Glow-Up Guide → Try AI Makeover → Save Results.

Shopping and professional bookings are optional follow-on actions, not the primary experience.

Bottom line: I would not scrap these designs. The visual identity is promising, and the Complete the Look page is a useful secondary feature. But the next screens the designer produces must be the Face Analysis and Glow-Up experience, because that is the product people will initially download GLAM AI to use.
