## 3. A crucial missing operational feature: merchant fulfillment

We've discussed merchant onboarding, Snap & List, location-based advertising and the 10% sales commission.

But the transaction experience also needs an operational layer.

For every purchase, GLAM AI must know whether the merchant can fulfill it.

Recommended order lifecycle

1. Order placed: Payment authorized or confirmed through the approved provider.

2. Merchant accepts: Stock, price and fulfillment availability confirmed.

3. Order prepared: Merchant packages the product or schedules pickup.

4. Shipped or collected: Tracking and delivery evidence recorded.

5. Completed: Transaction becomes eligible for final settlement under the agreed policy.

6. Commission reconciled: GLAM AI's 10% fee and merchant proceeds are recorded accurately.

Add automated handling for late dispatch, stock failures, partial shipments, refunds, chargebacks and merchant disputes.

Without this, a marketplace can look excellent while still delivering a poor buying experience.

## 4. One more feature: GLAM AI for Groups

This could be valuable for weddings, matric dances, traditional ceremonies and themed events.

Imagine a bride organizing outfits for six bridesmaids. GLAM AI could coordinate colors, modesty preferences, sizes, budgets and delivery dates without requiring everyone to wear identical clothing.

The same functionality could support couples, families, wedding parties and event teams.

I would classify Group Styling as Phase 2, not a launch blocker.

## 5. What I would not add before launch

I would deliberately postpone a few tempting features:

* A full social network with feeds and direct messaging.

* Live shopping streams.

* A proprietary delivery fleet.

* Cryptocurrency or a GLAM AI wallet.

* Complex merchant bidding auctions.

* Full international marketplace expansion on day one.

These features would increase engineering, compliance and operational complexity without necessarily improving the initial face-analysis-to-commerce experience.

My final recommendation: GLAM AI has enough planned features to be a serious competitor. The competitive advantage will now come from how seamlessly the features work together—not from adding another twenty modules.
