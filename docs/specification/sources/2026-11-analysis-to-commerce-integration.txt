## 5. Exact instruction to lock into Codex

GLAM AI — Mandatory Analysis-to-Commerce Integration

# GLAM AI — Mandatory Analysis-to-Commerce Integration

This is a permanent core product requirement and supersedes any previous instructions that suggest removing or deprioritizing commercial recommendations from AI analysis results.

GLAM AI remains an AI-first beauty, grooming and styling application. However, every relevant analysis, makeover, tutorial and personalized routine must include contextual product and professional recommendations from the platform's verified merchant, advertiser and service-provider listings.

## Required integration

For Face Analysis, Hair Analysis, Skin Appearance Analysis, Body & Fit Styling, Personal Color Analysis, Makeup Match and AI Makeover:

1. Generate personalized analysis and styling guidance.

2. Convert recommendations into structured product and service requirements.

3. Match requirements against approved merchant catalogs and professional listings.

4. Validate product suitability, variant, availability, region, currency and price.

5. Return relevant organic matches and separately labeled sponsored matches.

6. Show merchant names, verified prices, delivery/pickup options and purchase links where available.

7. Show professional profiles, service prices and booking options where supported.

8. Allow users to compare alternatives and save recommended items.

9. Provide Complete the Look for recommendations involving multiple products or services.

10. Track advertising impressions, clicks and attributable conversions with appropriate privacy safeguards.

## UI requirements

Every relevant analysis report must include:

* Your Results

* Personalized Recommendations

* Where to Buy / Book

* Sponsored Matches, clearly labeled

* Save Recommendations

* Complete the Look, when applicable

Do not force users to visit a separate shopping application or navigation tab to access recommended products.

## Commercial requirements

Advertisers and merchants use the unified GLAM AI business workspace with organization-scoped RBAC.

Approved merchants may list products and services. Approved advertisers may sponsor eligible placements.

The recommendation engine must prioritize genuine relevance and safety before advertising bids.

Do not modify underlying AI analysis findings based on advertiser payments.

Do not disclose private facial imagery, biometric data or sensitive inferred characteristics to advertisers.

Apply stricter age-appropriate advertising and commerce controls for minors.

## Acceptance criteria

A completed analysis must either:

* Show relevant, verified merchant/product/professional matches; or

* Clearly indicate that no verified matching listings are currently available.

No fake prices, stock levels, merchants, service availability or product claims are permitted.

Test the entire journey:

Analysis → Recommendation → Merchant Match → Product Detail → Purchase Handoff or Booking.

This requirement applies consistently to both consumer mobile and web applications.

Final decision: GLAM AI is AI analysis first, commerce integrated into every relevant result.

That gives us the best of both models: users come for facial analysis, hair analysis, personal color guidance and AI makeovers, while merchants and advertisers pay to help users obtain the products and services needed to achieve their chosen looks.
