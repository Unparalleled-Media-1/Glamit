### Correct Face Analysis journey

1. Start Analysis: From the Home hero or the Analyze tab.

2. Capture Photo: Guided selfie with lighting, angle and image-quality checks.

## 6. Instruction to correct the Codex and UI/UX briefs

GLAM AI — Mandatory Product Direction Correction

# GLAM AI — Mandatory Product Direction Correction

The previous GLAM AI specifications overemphasized shopping, fashion commerce and advertising.

This directive supersedes those navigation and prioritization decisions.

## Primary product identity

GLAM AI is first and foremost a global, inclusive AI-powered personal beauty, grooming, facial analysis and glow-up consultant.

The principal competitor benchmark is Glam Up – Perfect Your Look (App Store ID 6480331890).

Our product must provide comprehensive equivalents of the competitor's advertised core capabilities, with differentiated quality, inclusivity, personalization and user experience.

## Mandatory primary features

1. Guided selfie capture and upload.

2. Detailed facial feature analysis.

3. Face shape, eye shape, brow, lip and facial proportion guidance.

4. Skin tone and undertone estimation.

5. Personal color season analysis.

6. Personalized glow-up preview and actionable guide.

7. Makeup matching from inspiration images.

8. Step-by-step makeup tutorials and routines.

9. Hairstyle, hair color, wig and grooming simulations.

10. AI beauty and grooming advisor.

11. Saved analysis reports, routines and looks.

12. Optional personalized product and professional recommendations.

## Mobile navigation

Home | Analyze | AI Studio | AI Advisor | Profile

The Analyze tab must be a first-class destination and must provide direct access to Face Analysis and Color Analysis.

The Home screen must prominently feature a Start Face Analysis action.

## Web consumer navigation

Home | Face Analysis | Color Analysis | AI Studio | My Glow-Up Guide | AI Advisor

## Commercial functionality

Preserve Complete the Look, Screenshot to Shop, merchant catalogs, professional booking, creator commerce and advertising infrastructure.

However, make these secondary and contextual to the analysis, makeover and personal guidance experience.

Do not insert advertising into facial-analysis results in a way that compromises their integrity.

Sponsored product recommendations must be clearly identified.

## UX priorities

The main journey is:

Upload Selfie → Face Analysis → Personal Report → Glow-Up Guide → AI Makeover → Save and Follow Routine.

The secondary journey is:

Selected Look → Optional Product Recommendations → Shop or Book.

## Global inclusivity

Support diverse skin tones, facial features, hair textures, ages, genders, abilities, cultural styles and grooming preferences.

Do not generate attractiveness rankings or impose a single beauty standard.

Apply appropriate age-based safeguards, privacy protections and consent controls.

## Required action

Revise the master PRD, information architecture, mobile navigation, web navigation, screen inventory, UI/UX designs and phased backlog to reflect this corrected product hierarchy.

Maintain a feature-parity matrix against the competitor's documented capabilities.

Do not begin implementing the commerce-heavy navigation previously proposed.

Prioritize Face Analysis, Color Analysis, Glow-Up Guide, Makeup Match and AI Studio for the first commercial release.

The central product promise should now be:

“Understand your features. Discover your colors. Explore your look. Learn how to achieve it.”

That is a much closer fit to the competitor you originally identified, while leaving us room to build a more capable, inclusive platform with optional commerce later.
