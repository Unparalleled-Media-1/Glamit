# GLAM AI — Merchant Snap & List

AI-powered product listing, catalog enhancement and automatic matching

Absolutely. This should be a flagship feature of the Merchant workspace.

A merchant should be able to photograph a product using their phone, and GLAM AI should automatically create a professional product listing, enhance its presentation and make it discoverable through relevant consumer AI analysis results.

The goal is simple:

Snap → AI Enhance → Review → Publish → Get Matched → Sell.

## 6. Additional merchant features worth including

I would add four capabilities to make Snap & List commercially useful:

Smart Pricing Assistant: Show comparable public or participating merchant prices where reliable data exists. Let merchants choose their own prices.

Instant Campaign Creation: After publishing, offer an optional Promote This Product action that creates a sponsored listing campaign with a defined budget.

Catalog Health Dashboard: Highlight missing variants, low-quality photos, out-of-stock items, expired offers and products receiving impressions without clicks.

Multi-Channel Catalog Export: Allow merchants to export approved listings to supported external commerce platforms or social channels where integrations and platform permissions permit.

# GLAM AI — Merchant Snap & List AI Engine

Implement Snap & List as a first-class feature within the unified Merchant workspace on iOS, Android and web.

## Objective

Enable merchants to photograph a product and create a professional, searchable, AI-enhanced product listing with minimal manual effort.

## Required workflow

Capture or upload product images → AI enhancement → Product recognition → Listing generation → Merchant review → Validation → Publish → Catalog indexing → Consumer recommendation eligibility.

## AI features

* Background cleanup and image optimization.

* Lighting and framing enhancement.

* Product category and attribute extraction.

* Packaging text recognition and barcode lookup.

* Product title and description generation.

* Color, size, shade and variant suggestions.

* Multilingual descriptions.

* Search tags and visual embeddings.

* Duplicate listing detection.

* Catalog quality scoring.

* Optional promotional creative generation.

## Image integrity

Preserve the real product's appearance, shade, branding, proportions and packaging details.

Do not invent logos, certifications, ingredients, product features or misleading before-and-after results.

Store original images separately from enhanced derivatives.

Require merchant review before publication.

## Commerce integration

Published listings must be eligible for matching against:

* Face Analysis recommendations.

* Hair Analysis recommendations.

* Skin Appearance Analysis recommendations.

* Personal Color Analysis recommendations.

* Body and Fit Styling recommendations.

* Makeup Match.

* AI Makeover.

* Complete the Look.

Only approved, relevant and sufficiently verified listings may appear as purchasable recommendations.

Support organic recommendations and separately labeled sponsored placements.

## Merchant dashboard

Include:

* Snap & List.

* My Products.

* Draft Listings.

* Catalog Health.

* Inventory.

* Orders / Referral Leads.

* Promotions.

* Advertising.

* Analytics.

* Billing.

* Settings.

## Technical requirements

Use asynchronous AI processing, idempotent jobs, original/derivative media storage, merchant-owned catalog records, organization-scoped RBAC, audit logs and versioned listing approvals.

Implement human review and validation for uncertain or regulated product claims.

Support mobile camera capture and web bulk uploads.

## Acceptance criteria

A merchant can take a photo, generate an editable listing, verify all required facts and publish it.

A published product is indexed for relevant consumer AI recommendations without requiring a separate manual catalog entry.

An unverified or out-of-stock product must not be represented as an available verified match.

AI-generated advertising content must be distinguishable from factual product photography where necessary.

Implement automated tests for listing creation, AI processing, review, publishing, catalog matching, permissions and rollback.

My recommendation: Make Snap & List one of the first Merchant features released. It directly solves the problem of getting sufficient product inventory onto GLAM AI, which is essential for the analysis-to-commerce model to work.

The key is that a merchant can list a product in minutes, and GLAM AI can then match that product to relevant users whenever they perform a face, hair, color, makeup or styling analysis.
