Yes. This should become a major GLAM AI feature, and I would include it in the final product specification.

What you're describing goes beyond ordinary outfit recommendations. GLAM AI should act as a personal AI stylist that understands the occasion, dress code, cultural requirements, religious preferences, budget and location, then helps the user achieve the look through real merchants, fashion designers, tailors and rental providers.

The experience should be:

Describe your event → AI interprets the theme → Creates personalized looks → Try them on → Buy, rent or commission the outfit.

This also gives GLAM AI a valuable commercial opportunity beyond makeup and hair products.

## 4. Tailors and fashion designers need their own functionality

This is an important extension of the Beauty Professional workspace.

I would broaden it to Professional / Designer, with permission-based subtypes for hair stylists, makeup artists, tailors, dressmakers, fashion designers, alteration specialists and outfit-rental businesses.

A tailor's listing should include portfolio photographs, specialties, service area, price ranges, estimated production time, fitting arrangements, delivery options and available appointment slots.

For custom outfits, the workflow should be:

1. Generate design: AI creates a personalized outfit concept and visual references.

2. Request quotes: User selects verified tailors or designers to receive the design brief.

3. Confirm feasibility: Professional reviews fabrics, measurements, complexity and production time.

4. Compare offers: User compares price, portfolio, turnaround time and reviews.

5. Book and track: Once supported, manage fitting appointments, milestones and completion.

An AI-generated outfit must be treated as a design concept until a professional confirms that it can actually be manufactured.

I would rank Event Styling + Tailor Matching as a high-priority growth feature, particularly because it addresses purchases with a defined occasion, budget and deadline.

GLAM AI — AI Personal Stylist, Event Themes and Inclusive Dress Codes

# GLAM AI — AI Personal Stylist, Event Themes and Inclusive Dress Codes

Implement AI Personal Stylist as a major feature of the consumer AI Advisor and AI Studio.

## Core capabilities

1. Accept free-text styling requests for any event, occasion or theme.

2. Accept uploaded invitations, event posters and inspiration images.

3. Extract event names, dates, themes and dress codes, requesting user confirmation.

4. Where reliable official information exists, distinguish verified event themes from user-provided themes and AI suggestions.

5. Generate multiple personalized outfit concepts with accessories, hairstyle and makeup recommendations.

6. Support virtual try-on where technically feasible and appropriately consented to.

7. Allow users to specify budget, location, sizing, measurements, preferred colors, comfort, climate and delivery deadlines.

8. Integrate optional face, hair and personal color analysis results.

9. Match recommended outfits to real approved merchant listings.

10. Match custom designs to verified tailors, fashion designers, dressmakers and alteration professionals.

11. Support ready-made purchase, clothing rental, custom tailoring and alterations.

12. Provide quote requests, fitting appointments and booking workflows.

## Cultural and religious inclusivity

Implement a voluntary, user-controlled dress-preference system.

Support configurable coverage, opacity, garment fit, layering, headwear, fabric, ceremonial requirements and preferred clothing styles.

Include hijab-friendly and other modest-fashion options without assuming that all members of any religion follow identical requirements.

Support global cultural attire, including African, South Asian, Middle Eastern and other regional traditions.

Never infer religious identity from photographs or use religious preferences for targeted advertising without a valid, lawful basis.

Allow users to combine multiple style preferences.

## Professional marketplace

Extend the existing professional workspace to include tailors, fashion designers, dressmakers, alteration specialists and rental providers.

Support portfolios, service categories, custom quotes, fitting schedules, turnaround times, booking requests and reviews.

## Recommendation engine

Connect generated outfit requirements to merchant Snap & List catalogs and professional listings.

Rank by relevance, verified availability, size/fit, delivery deadline, price and user preferences.

Sponsored placements must be clearly identified and must not override essential compatibility requirements.

## Acceptance criteria

A user can describe an event theme, set their dress preferences and budget, receive personalized outfit concepts, and discover matching products or relevant professionals.

When no verified commercial match exists, show a transparent no-match state rather than fabricating availability or prices.

Preserve the existing AI-first beauty-analysis experience and unified role-switching architecture.

My recommendation: Add Personal Stylist as a prominent capability within AI Advisor, and Style My Event as a dedicated journey within it.

That makes GLAM AI useful not only for everyday glow-ups, but also for weddings, Eid celebrations, religious gatherings, graduations, matric dances, galas, traditional ceremonies and major fashion events—while connecting each styling recommendation to real commercial opportunities.
