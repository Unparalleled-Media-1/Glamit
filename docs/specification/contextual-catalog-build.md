# Contextual commerce and Merchant Snap & List

Current addendum, 5 November 2026 (Africa/Johannesburg). The [analysis-to-commerce directive](sources/2026-11-analysis-to-commerce-integration.txt) and [Snap & List directive](sources/2026-11-merchant-snap-and-list.txt) extend the authorized application build. Analysis stays primary; commerce belongs inside every relevant result, routine and tutorial, not behind a required Shop tab. Older wording describing commerce as optional must not hide contextual matching.

Every Face, Hair, Skin Appearance, Body & Fit, Color, Makeup Match and Makeover workspace has **Your Results → Personalized Recommendations → Where to Buy / Book**, separate **Best Matches / Sponsored Matches**, explicit no-match states, saving and Complete the Look. Uncomputed findings must not be turned into invented recommendations. Users may prepare and save their own requirements while providers are disconnected. Unknown or restricted age does not activate advertising or commerce.

Merchant Snap & List: original product images → optional qualified enhancement/recognition job → editable draft → merchant factual review → version-specific content approval → publication → transactional outbox/index projection → qualified matching. No AI output, ingredients, SPF, certification, efficacy, stock, price or shade compatibility is invented. Source originals and generated derivatives require separate rights/retention and storage; local previews are not uploaded or treated as catalog assets.

Canonical additions use the existing identity, organization capabilities, RBAC, idempotency, audit and outbox/migration history. Listings have immutable revisions, source/rights/fact confirmation, regulated-claim review, approved identifiers, offer expiry, stock/request availability, region/currency, safe HTTPS handoff and review/publish/withdraw/rollback transitions. Pending edits invalidate publication immediately; stale projections cannot restore eligibility. Background indexing reads current authoritative state, not old event payloads. Sponsored delivery remains empty until advertiser budget/consent/attribution adapters qualify; a bid cannot change analysis or make an ineligible candidate match.

Only server-reviewed current revisions with eligible organizations, current merchant source evidence, matching requirements, actual declared stock/request availability, region and currency can appear. Similarity tags never prove an Exact match. A verified source/SKU identifier can support Exact; otherwise label Similar. Unknown shipping/tax stays unknown; delivered totals use integer minor units only when every required cost is known. Services require professional capability and are quote requests, not confirmed bookings.

Review and publication require explicit action. A qualified merchant may write/review its own draft; only separately authorized fresh privileged content staff approve a revision. No client checkbox grants a verified capability, age assertion or staff authority. No public write endpoint activates commercial roles. No listings/price/availability or paid placements are seeded into the running application.

External auth, hosted DB, private storage, enhancement/recognition/embedding/OCR/translation vendors, catalogs, notifications, payment and ad delivery remain deferred under the user's connection instruction. A missing enhancement provider returns unavailable without creating a pretend completed job or spending credits. Local session planning works independently; canonical server workflows are tested against real PostgreSQL with explicit trusted test assertions, not live supplier claims.

Acceptance covers scoped listing create/revise/review/publish, rejected/stale/unreviewed cases, version rollback/withdrawal, outbox replay/order, exact versus similar, no-match, stale/out-of-stock/region/currency exclusion, organic/sponsored separation, private saved requirements, authorization/revocation, direct-client RLS, and mobile/web journey rendering. No public Git/environment publication or production database migration is authorized.

## Implemented contracts and current boundaries

- `POST /v1/organizations/{org}/catalog/listings` and `/bulk`: verified merchant/professional capabilities, draft facts, scoped case-insensitive source-SKU duplicate prevention, idempotency and all-or-nothing batch creation (maximum 50).
- `.../{id}/revisions`: immutable factual revisions and explicit merchant review; source SKU and business capability cannot silently change. Any edit immediately removes publication eligibility.
- `GET /v1/admin/catalog/review-queue` and revision review: fresh privileged content/Super Admin authority, readable merchant facts and a single immutable version-specific approval/rejection. Business ownership or finance access never grants approval authority. Staff context separately reports `catalog_review_enabled`; general privileged operations remain disabled.
- `.../{id}/publish`, `/withdraw`, `/rollback`, and `/catalog/publish-bulk`: source freshness, rights-confirmed validated original metadata, version approval and transaction/audit/outbox. Initial offer validity is bounded to 24 hours. Rollback copies an earlier reviewed version into a new unpublished revision needing fresh content approval; it never rewinds version numbers. Rental publication is deferred until deposits/returns/damage obligations qualify.
- Worker indexing consumes `catalog.changed.v1` with `SKIP LOCKED` and projects current canonical state. Replayed/out-of-order events cannot resurrect withdrawn or superseded offers. Consumer matching joins current publication, capability and media rights again; stale projections are not offer authority.
- `/v1/recommendations/matches` and listing detail revalidation: approved active source, all required tags, variant/SKU scope, available stock or professional request capacity, expiry, country/currency, age eligibility and latest qualified market policy. Unknown country policy is denied. A newer disabled market policy overrides an older enabled one. No market or merchant is seeded into the running app.
- Exact requires a content-verified identifier **and its source organization**. Same-looking products or colliding seller SKUs stay Similar. Used/refurbished condition is disclosed. Delivered totals include item, shipping, tax and other required costs only when all are known; unknown totals cannot pass a firm budget constraint. Service prices are indicative and require a feasibility/full-price quote; no booking is implied.
- Private saved requirements have owner-only create/list/delete, idempotent writes and deletion tombstones that erase cached response payloads. No face image, inferred identity or ad bid is accepted in a requirement.
- Enhancement request/status plus a qualified adapter protocol and leased worker: missing provider returns 503 with no pretend completed job or credit use. Provider retry keys remain stable across expired leases; stale workers cannot overwrite another lease. Output must declare completed features, separate derivatives from originals and disclose generated advertising. Suggestions never overwrite factual catalog revisions. Failed, stale, rights-revoked or unauthorized output is discarded; raw provider errors are not stored/logged.

The model covers original/derivative **metadata and lifecycle**, not an attached object store. Upload/read grants, binary storage/validation, real enhancement/recognition/OCR/translation/embedding adapters, model fidelity evaluation and any merchant-AI billing require the deferred connection/qualification stage. Tests use explicit in-process adapters and trusted assertions only; no fixture is a real integration.

Web and native share embedded recommendation sections and qualified-response rendering. Handoff/quote callbacks require fresh server detail validation; no destination opens automatically. The running disconnected app shows explicit no-match states, not sample merchants. Snap & List local photos are kept separate and unchanged; optional enhancement remains unavailable. Merchant facts/session drafts, duplicate checks, session catalog health, bounded web JSON import/export and native camera/picker code are implemented. Imports discard supplied identities, image links/counts and review attestations. Session notes are not persistent canonical listings, approved stock or live publication.

There is no live paid-placement auction, advertising charge, impression/conversion delivery, availability-notification delivery, purchase or booking integration in this update. Sponsored sections are structurally separate and empty until their adapters and permissions qualify. The permanent requirement to integrate those opportunities into results remains intact.
