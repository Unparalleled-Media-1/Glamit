# GLAM Beauty — permanent mobile-first product policy

Authority: user instruction, 8 November 2026 (Africa/Johannesburg). This governs future implementation, UX and release checks; it does not authorize production migrations, a public launch or invented store identifiers.

Consumers and creators use native iOS/Android as their primary daily experience. Prioritize analysis, Studio/try-on, styling/advice, Style Passport, wardrobe, discovery/shopping, saved looks, notifications and creator interactions. Public web acquires and converts users while preserving accessible alternatives, essential account recovery, privacy, support and existing working journeys.

Professionals/salons and affiliates use mobile and web; calendars, staff, reporting and management remain available on desktop. Merchants retain complete desktop inventory/order operations and native companions. Advertisers and administrators are web-first. Creators retain advanced desktop campaign/analytics tools. Never remove approved business functionality to promote app use.

Use tasteful contextual Get the GLAM App entry points, valid store links, QR codes and supported app links. Before publication, disclose availability and provide a working web fallback; no fake badges, dead store URLs or forced installation. After publication, validate actual store identifiers and use verified platform links; only supported, verified deferred deep linking may be claimed. Preserve the intended destination securely. Never pass bearer/refresh credentials through navigation URLs or claim automatic identity transfer without a validated authentication flow.

Retain shared backend, Supabase identity/RBAC/data and commercial rules. Reuse non-UI contracts, not DOM/WebView wrappers as native screens. Preserve the approved dark/cream/rose-gold design and exact supplied artwork.

Every consumer web journey needs an appropriate conversion opportunity without obstructing privacy, recovery, support or accessibility. Test valid destinations, installed/uninstalled fallback, target-screen routing, authenticated transitions and desktop business operations. Promotion readiness remains pending until the real store/app-link configuration exists.

Native iOS and Android must be independently tested. Browser prototypes, responsive screenshots, exported JavaScript, prebuilds, test doubles and typechecks do not certify native rendering, OS permissions, builds or installed-device behavior. Never call any platform 100% built without passing its applicable credential-independent requirements; deployed/device/provider activation and public-launch approval remain separate gates.
